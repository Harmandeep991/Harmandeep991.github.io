Questions about pricing

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan lets you schedule 5 buttons every month, forever, with no card. New accounts also get 30 days of Pro free to try everything.

What happens when my Pro trial ends?

You move to the free plan automatically. Anything you've already scheduled is still pressed. Repeating schedules pause until you go Pro again.

Will I be charged automatically?

Never. Pro is prepaid for 30 or 365 days. We email you 3 days before it ends, and you can renew in two clicks. If you buy while Pro is still active, the new time is added on top.

How can I pay?

Checkout is handled securely by PayU. You can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet. We never see your card or bank details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Pro isn't right for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund. See the refund and cancellation policy.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. Sign in to the extension with the same account on any Chrome browser you use yourself.

Do I get a receipt?

Every payment is emailed to you and listed on your account page. Need a company name on it? Email us.
