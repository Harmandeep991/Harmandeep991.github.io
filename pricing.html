Questions about pricing

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Sign up with your email or Google account and you get 30 days of every feature. We never ask for payment details until you choose a plan.

What happens when my trial ends?

Anything you've already scheduled still gets pressed. To schedule something new you'll need a plan. Your settings and history stay as they are.

Will I be charged automatically?

Never. Plans are prepaid for 30 or 365 days. We email you 3 days before your plan ends, and you can renew in two clicks. If you buy while a plan is still active, the new time is added on top.

How can I pay?

Checkout is handled securely by PayU. You can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet. We never see your card or bank details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Submit Later isn't right for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund. See the refund and cancellation policy for details.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. Sign in to the extension with the same account on any Chrome browser you use yourself.

Do I get a receipt?

Every payment is emailed to you and listed on your account page. Need a company name on it? Email us.
