Questions about pricing

Do I need to buy all three products?

No. Button Scheduler, Timed Forms and Webhook Scheduler have separate plans. Buy the one that fits your workflow and add another later from the same account.

What happens after the new-account trial?

No charge is made automatically. Each product returns to its free allowance or pauses paid-only capabilities until you purchase that product's plan.

Can I mix monthly and annual plans?

Yes. For example, you can keep Button Scheduler on an annual plan and use Webhook Scheduler for one month. Each product has its own access end date.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. Every plan is prepaid for 30 or 365 days. You decide whether to buy more time.

How can I pay?

Checkout is handled securely. You can pay by debit or credit card, UPI, net banking or wallet. We never see your card or bank details.

Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility and processing details are explained in the refund and cancellation policy.

Do I get a receipt?

Every payment is emailed to you and listed on your account page. Need a company name on it? Email us.
