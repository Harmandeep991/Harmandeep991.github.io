How it works

Three steps. No setup.

If you can click a button, you can schedule it. There's nothing to configure and no code.

Fill it in

Write the email, the WhatsApp message or the form exactly as you normally would.

Pick the button

Click the Submit Later icon → Pick a button to schedule, then click Send, Submit or Join. It's only selected, not pressed.

Choose the time

A quick pick or any date and time, to the second. It's pressed on time and you're notified.

Action Flows · included with Button Scheduler

Turn a routine website task into a reusable flow.

Prepare a Gmail draft, fill an internal form or select a weekly report. Record the clicks and typing once, review the steps, then run the flow yourself or set a schedule.

  • Edit fields, replace a target and wait for a page to load.
  • Add conditions, repeat steps or use a different value on each run.
  • Use Ask me to review a draft or complete a private field yourself.

Private by design

What Submit Later can and can't see

It can see

  • Only the tab where you click the icon and pick a button: it reads just enough of that page to find the button again and press it at your time.
  • For Action Flows, reopen-and-press and WhatsApp, the sites you explicitly allow when Chrome asks. Recorded steps and ordinary field values stay on your computer.

It never

  • Read your other tabs, your browsing history or what you type elsewhere.
  • Send what you schedule (pages, messages, contacts) to our server: it stays on your computer.
  • Create schedules or flows on your behalf: you choose the action, timing and steps.

Extension schedules stay in your browser. Timed Forms and Webhook Scheduler store only the configurations and run records needed to provide those server-side tools. Read the full privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to keep the tab open?

For buttons in a form you've filled in, yes, so what you typed is kept. With Pro's "Reopen & press", Submit Later opens the page itself at the time, so you can close the tab. Chrome needs to be running either way.

What if my computer goes to sleep?

While something is scheduled, Submit Later keeps your computer awake (the screen can still turn off). Closing a laptop lid still puts it to sleep, so leave it open or plugged in with the lid up.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. Guest access and Pro both let you schedule WhatsApp Web messages to any number or imported contact, once or on repeat: to up to 20 people at a time, several messages each, with photos and videos up to 16 MB. The 9 shared guest schedules count each recipient and repeat occurrence separately. Stay signed in to WhatsApp Web on that computer. Read more.

Is my data safe?

Extension schedules, contacts and message content stay in your browser. Server-side products store the form or webhook configuration you ask us to run, along with operational records. See the privacy policy.

I don't see Submit Later's notifications

Submit Later tells you the result of each scheduled press with a Chrome notification, but your computer decides whether to show it. If you see nothing:
Mac: System Settings → Notifications → Google Chrome → turn on Allow notifications and choose Alerts (so they stay until you click). Turn off Focus / Do Not Disturb.
Windows 11: Settings → System → Notifications → turn on Notifications and Google Chrome, and turn off Do not disturb. Windows 10: Settings → System → Notifications & actions → turn on Google Chrome, and turn off Focus assist.
Chrome: keep Chrome open; notifications come from it. If you use several Chrome profiles, they show for the profile where Submit Later is installed.
You won't miss anything either way: every result is in the extension's popup under Last result.

What can't it do?

It can't solve CAPTCHAs, sign in for you, or bypass a site's rules, and it won't send bulk messages. It presses buttons you could press yourself.
