The problem

Your hosting doesn't have cron.
Running your own is overkill.

Vercel, Netlify, Railway, Render free tier — none of them have reliable built-in cron. Setting up a VPS just to run a cronjob costs $5–20/month and someone has to maintain it.

☁️

Serverless has no cron

Vercel's built-in cron requires a Pro plan ($20/mo). Netlify's needs a paid tier too. You're paying for deployment hosting, not a scheduler.

🖥️

A server just for cron is waste

Running a $6/mo VPS to do 0 9 * * * curl https://… once a day. Then monitoring it. Then fixing it when the server reboots.

🔗

No-code tools can't schedule URLs

Bubble, Webflow, Glide — they all support webhooks but have no native scheduler. You end up using Zapier's Schedule trigger just to hit a URL.

🔇

"Did it actually fire?"

curl in cron gives you nothing. No logs. No alert when it fails. No history. You find out a job stopped working when something breaks in production.

FAQ

Common questions.

Try it free →
How precise is the timing?

Jobs fire within ±2 seconds of the scheduled time. Our worker polls every 30 seconds and fires immediately on pickup. For most use cases (reports, cache invalidation, reminders) this is more than precise enough. If you need sub-second precision, contact us.

What happens if the request times out?

We wait up to 30 seconds for a response. If it times out, it's logged as a failure. On Starter and above, the retry logic kicks in automatically.

Can I pass authentication headers?

Yes. Add any headers you need — Authorization, X-API-Key, Content-Type, anything. On Starter and above, you can store them as secrets (encrypted, not visible after saving).

Does my server need to be publicly reachable?

Yes — we fire the request from our servers, so your endpoint needs to be on a public HTTPS URL. Local development URLs (localhost) won't work, but services like ngrok give you a public URL for testing.

Can I use this instead of Vercel's cron?

Yes. Create a job pointing at your Vercel function URL. We fire it on the schedule you set. No Vercel Pro plan required. You can even fire the same job to multiple environments (staging, production) as separate jobs.

What counts as an "execution"?

One outgoing HTTP request. A job that runs daily uses 30 executions per month. The free plan's 200 executions/month supports about 6 daily jobs or 1 job running every 3 hours.
