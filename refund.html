Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

7-day money-back guarantee

If Submit Later isn't right for you, email us within 7 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full. No questions asked.

We always refund when

  • you were charged more than once for the same plan,
  • you were charged an amount different from the price shown at checkout,
  • the payment was made without your authorisation, or
  • you paid but your plan didn't activate and we couldn't fix it within 1 working day.

For later payments (renewals), we also consider any request made within 7 days of the payment, for example if the extension stopped working on a site you rely on.

How to ask for a refund

Email singh.harmandeep09@gmail.com from your account's email address with the payment date and the transaction ID from your receipt or account page. We reply within 2 working days.

Processing time

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. When a plan is refunded, its time is removed from your account.

Failed payments

If money left your account but the payment shows as failed, your bank or PayU normally reverses it automatically within 5–7 working days. If it doesn't, email us with the details and we'll help.

Cancellation

Plans are prepaid and never renew automatically, so there's nothing to cancel and you'll never be charged again without choosing to pay. Your plan simply stays active until its end date. The free trial needs no cancelling either; it just ends. You can delete your account at any time from your account page.
