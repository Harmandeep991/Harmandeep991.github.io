Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Contributions are voluntary

Submit Later is free. Contributions are optional, one-time payments that support its development and don't purchase goods or extra features. Because of this, contributions are generally non-refundable.

When we do refund

We'll refund a contribution in full if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same contribution,
  • you were charged an amount different from the one you chose, or
  • the payment was made without your authorisation.

We'll also consider any other request made within 7 days of the payment.

How to request a refund

Email singh.harmandeep09@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment with the date, amount, payment method and transaction reference. We reply within 2 working days.

Processing time

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it. Refunds for payments made in another currency are made in INR, the currency we're paid in, so the amount you receive may differ slightly due to exchange rates and fees charged by your card provider.

Cancellation

Contributions are one-time payments, not subscriptions, so there's nothing to cancel and you'll never be charged again automatically. If you started a payment by mistake, you can close the checkout page before paying.
