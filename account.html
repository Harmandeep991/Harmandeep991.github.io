Billing questions

Will I be charged automatically?

Never. Plans are prepaid. We email you 3 days before your plan ends, and you decide whether to buy again.

What happens when my trial or Pro ends?

You move to the free plan (5 schedules a month). Anything already scheduled is still pressed; repeating schedules pause until you're Pro again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund within 7 days of your first payment. See the refund policy.

Need help?

Ask for more time, a refund, or help with a problem. We reply here and by email.

    Troubleshooting

    It says the extension isn't installed, but it is

    Make sure it's turned on in chrome://extensions, then click Check again. This page only detects the extension in Google Chrome on a computer.

    It's signed in to a different account

    Click Connect extension here to switch it to this account.

    I paid but the extension still says my trial ended

    Open the extension's popup and click Already paid? Check again. It refreshes your plan from our server.
