Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains how the Submit Later Chrome extension ("the extension") and this website handle your information. Submit Later is operated by Harmandeep Singh, B-34/6378, Street No. 3, Jagat Nagar, Jassian Road, Haibowal Kalan, Ludhiana (East), Ludhiana, Punjab 141001, India ("we", "us").

The short version

The extension doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything it stores stays in Chrome on your own computer. This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or trackers. The home page loads approved reviews from our Google Apps Script; Google may log that request like any web request.

What the extension stores, on your device only

For each button you schedule: the page's address and title, the visible text of the button, a technical locator for it, the scheduled time and the result. The last 50 results are kept so you can see them. Your settings (including your colour theme) are stored with Chrome's sync storage, so if you use Chrome sync they follow your Google account like any other Chrome setting. You can clear history in the extension, and everything is deleted when you uninstall it.

What the extension reads on web pages

It runs only on a tab where you start the button picker yourself. It reads just enough of that page to let you pick a button, check that required fields are filled, and press the button at the time you chose. It doesn't read or store what you type into forms.

Reliable clicking

When "Reliable clicking" is on (the default), the extension uses Chrome's debugger permission only to send a single mouse click to the button you picked, at the time you scheduled, and then detaches immediately. It isn't used to read page content or network traffic.

Problem reports

A problem report is sent only when you press "Submit problem". It goes to a Google Apps Script operated by us, which emails it to our inbox. Before sending you can see everything it contains: your description, any screenshot links and email address you choose to add, and optional technical details (extension version, browser, language, time zone, extension settings and the site's name; never the full page address or form contents). We use reports only to provide support and fix problems, and delete them when no longer needed.

Reviews

If you share your experience from the extension, your star rating, comment and name (or "Anonymous") are stored in a Google Sheet operated by us, along with whether you allowed it to be shown publicly. Only reviews you allowed and we approved are shown in the extension and on this website, with the rating, comment, name or "Anonymous" and month. To have a review removed, email us.

Contributions and the supporters wall

Contributions are processed by PayU, Razorpay or your UPI app under their own privacy policies. We never see your card or bank details; we receive only the payment confirmation (amount, date, reference) from the provider. If you choose to add yourself to the supporters wall, the name (or "Anonymous"), amount, currency, method, message and optional transaction reference you enter are stored in a Google Sheet operated by us. Only approved entries are shown, and only the name or "Anonymous", the amount if you allowed it, the message and the date. Transaction references are never published.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete any information you've sent us (problem reports or supporters-wall entries) by emailing singh.harmandeep09@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Children

Submit Later isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the extension's release notes.
