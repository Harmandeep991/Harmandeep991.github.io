The problem

what time it is.

Can students bypass the deadline by changing their browser clock?

No. We check the time on our server — never in the browser. A student can set their system clock to any year they want; they'll still see the countdown or the closed page, because we decide what page they get, not their device.

Do I need to rebuild my Google Form?

No. You paste your existing Google Form URL, set the window and share the SubmitLater link. Your form stays exactly as it is. We just control when people can reach it.

What do students see before the form opens?

A branded countdown page with your organization name, the form name and a live countdown timer. On Pro and above you can add your logo and choose a color. On Pro, they also see the option to join the pre-queue.

What happens if I update my Google Form URL?

Update it in your dashboard in one click. Your SubmitLater link stays the same — the one you already shared still works.

How does the pre-queue actually submit to Google Forms?

Using the Google Forms API. Queued entries are submitted in queue order at the exact millisecond the form opens. We handle rate limiting and retry failures. Students get a confirmation as soon as their entry is submitted.

Is the submission timestamp in Google Forms reliable?

Yes. Since we submit to Google Forms at the moment of opening, all pre-queue entries have a submission timestamp at or within seconds of the open time. Entries submitted directly during the window have normal Google Forms timestamps, all of which fall within your window by definition — because we block access outside it.
