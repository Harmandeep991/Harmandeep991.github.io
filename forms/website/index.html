FAQ

What to know
before you publish.

Does changing a device clock affect the timed link?

No. Submit Later uses server time to decide whether its link shows a countdown, redirects, or displays the closed page. Device time does not change that link state.

Do I need to rebuild my Google Form?

No. Paste the URL of an existing Google Form, choose the window, and share the new timed URL. The form itself stays hosted by Google.

What does a visitor see at each stage?

Before opening, they see a live countdown and scheduled time. During the window, the link redirects to your Google Form. After closing, it shows a closed message and stops redirecting.

What happens if I update my Google Form URL?

Edit the form in your dashboard and save the new destination. The Submit Later short link stays the same, so previously published copies continue to work.

Can someone use the original Google Form URL directly?

Yes. Submit Later controls only the timed URL it creates. If someone already has the original Google Form URL, access follows the settings you configured in Google Forms. Share the timed URL and keep the original private until you are ready.

Does closing the timed link stop Google Form responses?

No. The timed link stops redirecting, but it does not switch off response collection inside Google Forms. If you require the underlying form itself to stop accepting responses, change that setting in Google Forms as well.

What does the view count measure?

It counts visits to the timed URL across countdown, open, and closed states. A view is not proof that a visitor submitted the Google Form.
