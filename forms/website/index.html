A few useful details

Before you get started

Read the complete walkthrough →
Will people still be able to use the original Google Form URL?

An embed does not make that URL secret. Managed imports synchronize Google’s accepting-responses setting with your schedule. Check the original form when testing the deadline; legacy links alone do not close it.

Where do responses go?

Respondents submit directly to Google Forms. Submit Later manages your page, questions and response window; it does not store their answers.

Do I need the Chrome extension or a second account?

No extension is needed. Use your existing Submit Later account. Timed Forms runs in the shared dashboard, and its response window is managed by the server.
