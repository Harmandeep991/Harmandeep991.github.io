FAQ

What to know
before you publish.

Connect a Google Form →
What permission does Submit Later request?

You connect Google separately from sign-in and authorize access for managed Google Forms. Submit Later uses that permission to read the selected form, edit supported questions and change whether it accepts responses. You can disconnect it from the dashboard.

Which questions can I edit?

Short text, paragraph, multiple-choice, checkbox and dropdown questions can be added, edited or removed. Other Google Forms item types are imported as read-only so their structure is not damaged.

What does a visitor see at each stage?

Before opening, they see your branded countdown. During the window, the Google Form appears inside the same page. After closing, the page shows the closed state and Submit Later tells Google to stop accepting responses.

Can I put the timed form on my own website?

Yes. Copy the iframe snippet from the dashboard into your site. It loads the same background, colours, countdown, live embedded form and closed state you configured in Submit Later.

Is the original Google Form URL hidden?

No iframe can make its source URL secret; a visitor can inspect it with browser tools. The protection comes from changing Google's own response setting with the schedule, so knowing the responder URL does not bypass a successfully synchronized closing state.

Does response access change at the exact second?

The Submit Later page changes from server time at the scheduled boundary. Google's response setting is updated over its cloud API and can take a brief moment to synchronize. The dashboard shows connection or synchronization problems that need attention.

Do wrapper colours change the Google Form fields?

No. Your background, accent colour and organization name style the Submit Later wrapper. Google renders the embedded questions and controls, and continues to receive the submitted responses.
