Answers before you email

Quick checks for the questions we see most often.

The scheduled action did not run. What should I send?

Open the extension options and choose Report a problem. Add the scheduled time, website and outcome you expected. The diagnostic details help us distinguish a browser timing issue from a page that changed.

My paid plan is not showing on my account.

Email us from the address used for your Submit Later account and include the transaction ID from your payment receipt. Do not send card, bank or UPI credentials.

How do I request a refund?

Send a billing support email with your account address and transaction ID. Eligibility and processing details are explained in the refund and cancellation policy.

I reinstalled the extension. Is my plan still available?

Yes. Sign in with the same Submit Later account to restore the plan attached to that account. Locally stored schedules and contacts may not follow you to a new browser or profile.

A better bug report

These details make it easier to reproduce the issue:

  • The product and browser you were using
  • The steps that led to the error
  • The exact error message or a screenshot
  • The date, time and timezone of a scheduled run

Formal grievance

Email submitlater.review@gmail.com with the subject Formal grievance. Include your account email, a clear description and any relevant transaction ID.

We acknowledge formal complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days.
