How it works

Set it once, then get on with your day

Open the page, click Watch

Click the Submit Later icon, then Watch this page for changes. Chrome asks to allow it on that one site, because watching reloads and reads the page.

Say what to wait for

Text that appears ("In stock"), text that disappears ("No slots available"), or any change in an area you click, like a price or a list. Choose how often to check and when to stop.

Get rung when it happens

A chime, a notification, the tab brought forward and the spot highlighted. Or have it press a button you picked, once, with a real click.

What it can watch

Words, areas and the button after

Text that appears

"In stock", "Book now", "Results declared". Limit it to one part of the page if the words appear elsewhere too.

Text that disappears

"Sold out", "No slots available", "Coming soon". It rings when the words are gone.

Any change in an area

Click a price, a queue number, a list of listings or a status, and it rings when anything there changes.

Press a button when found

Add to cart, Book, Register: Submit Later presses the button you picked, once, as soon as it shows up.

Power features

Everything an auto-refresh extension does, and more

Auto-refresh, free

Every second to every hour, a random time in a range, start at a set time, only between set hours on chosen days, stop after N reloads or when you use the page. Hard refresh, and "never resubmit a form". Alt+Shift+R starts or stops it.

Smarter matching

Several keywords (any or all), regular expressions, CSS selectors or XPath, in the visible text or the page source, limited to one area if you like. Save sets as templates. Write it with AI writes the selector or pattern from a description (5 a day free, 50 with Pro).

Alerts anywhere

An email to your account address, or a webhook to Discord, Slack, Telegram or your own URL: 10 of each a day on a free account, 200 with Pro.

Live, background and automatic

Check the moment the page changes without reloading, keep checking with the tab closed (it opens the page in the background), or start by itself on pages that match an address pattern.

Page actions and scripts

Ready-made actions: scroll down, click "Load more", close cookie banners, expand sections, keep the session alive. With Pro, add your own JavaScript.

Sync and profiles

Your settings, templates, auto-start rules and profiles on all your computers (3 free, 10 with Pro). Contacts, flows and messages stay on each computer.

FAQ

Watching questions

How often can it check?

Every second to every hour in an open tab (at most once a minute with the tab closed). Tick Vary the timing a little to spread the checks between 70% and 130% of that, so they look less regular. Chrome versions before 120 check at most once a minute.

Does the tab need to stay open?

Yes. A watch reloads and reads the tab you started it in. Keep Chrome running and the computer awake; you can use other tabs meanwhile. Closing the tab or restarting Chrome stops the watch.

Will it reload while I'm typing?

No. A check waits if you typed or clicked in that tab in the last 20 seconds, or if a field there has text in it. It also never reloads a tab that has a button press scheduled.

What if the page updates by itself?

Open More options and choose Don't reload. Submit Later then just reads the page on each check. Hard refresh does the opposite and skips the browser's cache.

Can it keep watching after it rings?

Yes. Tick Keep watching after it happens, and it rings again on each new change. Stop it any time from the bar on the page or the extension popup.

What about CAPTCHAs and "slow down" pages?

The watch pauses and rings you. Solve it or wait, then resume from the page bar or the popup. Submit Later never solves CAPTCHAs.

How much does it cost?

Starting a watch uses one schedule: one of your 12 guest tries, or one of the free plan's monthly schedules. Button Scheduler Pro includes unlimited watches. The checks themselves are always free. See pricing.

Is anything sent to Submit Later?

No. The page is read in your browser, and only a short fingerprint of the text is kept on your computer to compare with next time. See the privacy policy.

How is this different from an auto-refresh extension?

It refreshes too, but it is built around what happens next: it rings you with the spot highlighted, can press the button for you with a real click, pauses on CAPTCHAs, and sits alongside scheduled presses, reminders, WhatsApp scheduling and Action Flows in one extension.
